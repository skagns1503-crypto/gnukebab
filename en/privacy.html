Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

GNU Kebab ("we") handles the personal information of people who use gnukebab.co.kr and the group order page (gnukebab.co.kr/order) in line with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it and how we delete it.

1. What we collect and why

WhenInformationPurpose
Sign-up and sign-inName, mobile number (verified by text), group name (if given), ID and password (if you sign up with an ID; the password is stored hashed)Identifying you, showing your orders, signing you in again
Sign-in with Kakao, Naver or GoogleThe account ID, name (nickname) and email address (if provided) that each company sharesRecognising returning users, filling in your name
OrdersRecipient name and mobile number, delivery address and drop-off spot, delivery date and time, menu, quantity and amount, requests, cash receipt number (if requested)Preparing and delivering food, checking payment, order status texts, issuing cash receipts
InquiriesName, group name, mobile number, your messageReplying to you
ReviewsStar rating, review text, order numberReview points, showing reviews on our website without names
Promotions (optional)Mobile number, consentDiscount and new menu news. You can order without agreeing

We do not collect personal information when you only browse the website.

2. How long we keep it

Account information is kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, we remove your name, mobile number and email address right away.

Records that the law requires us to keep are stored separately only for the required period.

RecordPeriodLaw
Contracts, cancellations and payments5 yearsAct on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce
Complaints and disputes3 yearsAct on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce
Tax-related records such as cash receipts5 yearsFramework Act on National Taxes

3. How we delete it

When the period ends or the information is no longer needed, we delete it without delay. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered; paper is shredded or burned.

4. Sharing with others

We do not give your personal information to anyone else, except when an authority requests it through a legal procedure.

7. Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop us processing it.

Tap "Delete account" in your member details on the order page to delete it immediately, or contact us by email or phone.

8. How we protect it

Passwords are stored hashed so the original cannot be read. Only the shop's operating account can open the records, and all pages use encrypted connections (https).

9. Browser storage

We use your browser's storage on your device to remember your sign-in and recent delivery address. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Clearing the storage in your browser settings signs you out.
