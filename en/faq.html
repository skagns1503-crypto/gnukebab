FAQ

Where can I eat halal food in Jinju?

GNU Kebab uses halal-certified beef and chicken. Muslim students and international guests are welcome.

Is there a good place to eat alone near Gyeongsang National University?

We are 519 m from the north gate of the Gajwa campus. One kebab is a full meal — many guests even split it into two.

Do you take group orders or event lunch boxes?

Yes. No need to worry about who can eat what. We also cater for public offices and school events — order on our Group Orders page.

Can I take out or get delivery?

Dine-in / takeout / Naver order and delivery are all available.

Where can I park?

There is no private parking. Please use the Gaho 1 public street parking (1407-1 Gajwa-dong).

What are your hours?

Open daily 9 AM – 10 PM. Closed on Tuesdays.

Is it spicy? What should I try first?

Every kebab comes with both our tangy white sauce and spicy red sauce, made to suit Korean tastes, so first-timers enjoy it too. Extra sauce or sauce on the side is free — just add it to your order request.

Where does the meat come from?

Both beef and chicken come from Indonesia / Australia / Brazil. The full origin list is at the bottom of the Menu page.

Where is kebab from? What style do you make?

Kebab comes from Turkey. At GNU Kebab we grill halal-certified beef and chicken and wrap it in a tortilla with vegetables and our white and red sauces — Turkish style.

Can children eat it?

For children we suggest the Mini Kebab — it fits in one hand. Our recipes suit Korean tastes so kids enjoy them too.

Can I get extra sauce or sauce on the side?

Yes. Extra sauce or sauce on the side is free — just add it to your order request.

Can you leave out ingredients like cucumber or onion?

Yes. Tell us in store or add it to your delivery request and we will leave it out.

Can I eat in the morning? Do you deliver in the morning?

Yes. We open at 9 AM every day (closed Tuesdays) — dine-in and delivery both start in the morning.

What is baklava?

Baklava is a traditional Turkish dessert filled with nuts and soaked in honey — crisp outside and moist inside. It tastes a little like Korean walnut cakes. We do not sell it now but plan to give it out at a Cost Price Day event.

What is the difference between beef / chicken and mix?

Chicken is light and beef is rich. Want both? Choose Mix — half and half. Prices: Chicken 8,900 / Mix 9,900 / Beef 10,900 won (Handmade Kebab).

What is the price range?

Handmade Kebab 8,900–10,900 won / Mini Kebab 6,500–7,500 won / Hot dogs from 5,500 won.

How many calories and how much protein?

One Handmade Kebab is about 590–700 kcal with 34–43 g of protein. A Mini Kebab is about 540–580 kcal (calculated from the recipe).

How do I reheat a kebab?

Wrap it in foil and heat it in a 180°C oven for 5–7 minutes to keep the tortilla from getting soggy. In a microwave wrap it in a damp paper towel for about 1 minute — the tortilla may get a little soft.

Is one kebab filling? How big is it?

One kebab is 290–320 g by ingredient weight — a full meal. Many guests even split it into two.
