FAQ

Where can I eat halal food in Jinju?

GNU Kebab uses halal-certified beef and chicken. Muslim students and international guests are welcome.

Is there a good place to eat alone near Gyeongsang National University?

We are 519 m from the north gate of the Gajwa campus. One kebab is a full meal — many guests even split it into two.

Do you take group orders or event lunch boxes?

Yes. No need to worry about who can eat what. We also cater for public offices and school events — order on our Group Orders page.

Can I take out or get delivery?

Dine-in / takeout / Naver order and delivery are all available.

Where can I park?

There is no private parking. Please use the Gaho 1 public street parking (1407-1 Gajwa-dong).

What are your hours?

Open daily 9 AM – 10 PM. Closed on Tuesdays.

Is it spicy? What should I try first?

The white sauce is mild and the red sauce is spicy. First time? Go with the white sauce.

Where does the meat come from?

Both beef and chicken come from Indonesia / Australia / Brazil. The full origin list is at the bottom of the Menu page.
